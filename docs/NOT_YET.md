# ELECTRO-HOBBY-3D-UPDATER: not yet

Good ideas that are not in the current version. Writing them here keeps anybody from implementing them early or twice.

| Idea | Why not yet | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|
| Update of everything across the ecosystems | Every updater asks for its own confirmation and the family decided that a person updates one project at a time | the updaters share a way to confirm a batch and the person asks for it |
| Updating the updaters from the window | An updater that replaces itself can break its own installation, as the one of A.R.M.O.R. still does | every updater can update itself without breaking |
| A signature check of its own | The check belongs to each updater and doubling it would let the two disagree | the updaters publish one shared way to verify a revision |
| The window showing the versions | The command `versions` is enough for now and the window has not been looked at on every system | a person has used the window on the three systems |
| A fifth kind of project | A project belongs to an ecosystem by having its manifest; nothing in this tool lists projects | a new ecosystem with its own updater exists |
